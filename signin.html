Sign in / On the host

The Members Club runs on the host — sign in to your account.

Sign-in and payments are handled by the platform that hosts this site. The site ships a real sign-in form backed by the host's session; you sign in with email + password, or request a one-tap magic link. You can end any session, and one email gets you a copy or a full delete of your data.

  • Real auth. Email + password, or magic link to your inbox. The form below POSTs to the host's auth endpoint and lands you on account.html.
  • Real billing. Tiers (Free / Member / Patron) are priced on the host; receipts come from the host's billing adapter and land in your email.
  • Privacy floor. The host stores email + handle only. No ad pixels, no third-party scripts on the public log. Member downloads arrive as private links that expire - safe by design.
  • No fake buttons. If the form fails, the form fails honestly. There are no dead ends here.

On the host. This page is wired to the host's auth + billing API. The visual layer (form, copy, status, theme) is shipped by the static site; the session, password reset, and member-gated delivery are handled by the host. See README.md §"Auth architecture" for the wiring contract.
