HOW THE CLUB WORKS

Sign-in and payments run on the platform that hosts this site; content stays on the site.

The Members Club is the first surface on this site that requires an account. The platform hosting this site handles sign-in and payments. The site shows the pages; the platform keeps the accounts and memberships. Privacy floor: we store just your email and a public nickname - no ads, no trackers. Member downloads arrive as private, expiring links.
